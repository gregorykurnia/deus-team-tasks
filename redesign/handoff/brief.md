# Design brief

## Who uses it
Who the people are, their roles, which devices they use and in what situations.

## Language
Which language or languages the interface uses.

## Personality
Calm or editorial? Dense and tool-like? Playful? Warm or clinical?

## References
Two or three apps you admire that are not this one, and what you admire in each.

## Constraints
Phone and large displays, installable, light and dark mode, signed-in or not, who can see what.

## Branding
What, if anything, is kept (for example only the name). Everything else is open.

## Permission to challenge the model
Whether the designer may propose merging, splitting or removing concepts (flagged for your decision, never adopted silently).
