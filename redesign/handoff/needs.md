# Needs

Design gaps found after the design was approved, written as job stories.

| ID | Need (job story) | Raised in | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| N1 | When I look back at finished tasks, or at when a flow last changed, and that date was never recorded (work finished or changed before dates were kept), I want to see that the date isn't known rather than a wrong date, and still find that work when I narrow by when it was finished, so that I can trust the dates I do see. | Step 6 | open |
| N2 | When a colleague's changes arrive while I'm working, I want to know that changes just came in, even though nobody signs in and so nobody can tell who made them, so that things changing on their own don't confuse me. | Step 6 | open |
| N3 | When I start my day and search for a word that could belong to a task, a company or qualification notes, I want to find matches from all three in one place, so that I don't have to search each area on its own. | Step 6 | open |
| N4 | When I open the app and it hasn't heard back from the team's shared data yet, I want to know it is still connecting, rather than being told everyone is up to date or that I have no connection, so that I know whether what I see is current and whether my changes can be saved yet. | Phase 2 review | open |
| N5 | When part of the team's data can't be loaded for a reason other than having no connection, I want to be told that part didn't load, rather than see zero totals or an invitation to start from nothing, so that I don't mistake missing data for no data. | Phase 2 review | open |
| N6 | When I open a task from a link that no longer leads anywhere (someone deleted the task), or a colleague deletes the task while I have it open and am changing it, I want to be told the task is gone, and not lose sight of what I typed, so that I'm not left wondering whether my change was saved. | Task 3.1 | open |
