# D — Glass

**Idea:** frosted-glass surfaces floating over a soft, colourful backdrop (periwinkle, lilac, blush, mint). The standout is a violet hero card with a progress ring and a one-line summary of what needs you.
**Moving around:** a floating glass side bar on large screens (areas, plus task groups with important ones starred); on phones, a floating glass tab bar with a raised add button in the middle.
**Density:** medium-airy. Tasks are grouped by urgency (overdue, due in 3 days, later), with today's calendar and each person's load beside them.
**Type:** Plus Jakarta Sans, extra-bold page titles and bold task names.
**Colour:** indigo-to-violet accent gradient. Work kinds are violet (prospects), teal (clients) and blue (internal), each with its own tint; soft red and amber pills show urgency.
