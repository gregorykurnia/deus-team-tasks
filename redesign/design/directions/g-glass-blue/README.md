# G — Glass in calm blue (mix of D and F)

**Idea:** D's frosted panels over a soft backdrop, recoloured from violet to a calm blue. The violet hero block is now a light frosted card: a one-line summary on the left and F's "Next two weeks" deadline chart on the right.
**Moving around:** as in D. A floating glass side bar on large screens (areas plus task groups); on phones, a floating tab bar with a raised add button.
**Density:** medium-airy. Tasks grouped by urgency (overdue, due in 3 days, later), with today's calendar and each person's load beside them.
**Type:** Plus Jakarta Sans, extra-bold titles.
**Colour:** pale sky-and-aqua backdrop, blue #2F6BEA accent. Prospects blue, clients green, internal slate; soft red and amber for urgency.
