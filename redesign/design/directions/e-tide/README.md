# E — Tide

**Idea:** a deep blue-to-cyan colour band carries the headline ("9 things need you today"), today's events and the urgency counts. White columns overlap the band, one each for prospects, clients and internal work.
**Moving around:** pill-shaped area tabs inside the band on large screens; on phones, a white bottom bar with a raised add button, and Prospects / Clients / Internal tabs to switch columns.
**Density:** medium. Three columns of rounded task cards; overdue cards are tinted red.
**Type:** Manrope, a 56 px extra-bold headline and bold card titles.
**Colour:** ocean blue with a cyan glow. Prospects blue, clients green, internal violet; red and amber for urgency.
