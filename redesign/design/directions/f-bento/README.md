# F — Bento

**Idea:** today laid out as a grid of rounded tiles in soft pastels. The standout is a lilac "Next two weeks" chart of deadlines per day, coloured by kind of work, with the busiest day called out.
**Moving around:** a floating pill rail of icons with labels on large screens; on phones, a dark floating dock with an add button.
**Density:** medium-high, but broken into tiles: the task list, the chart, overdue, gone quiet and today's calendar each get their own tile.
**Type:** Figtree, extra-bold date heading and big numbers in the small tiles.
**Colour:** cool grey page with white, lilac, peach, butter and sky tiles; dark ink buttons. Prospects violet, clients green, internal blue; red for overdue.
