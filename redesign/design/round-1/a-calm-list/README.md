# A — Calm list

**Idea:** a quiet, personal to-do list. "Today" is one calm column of tasks, split into prospects, clients and internal work, with urgency written in words.
**Moving around:** a fixed side bar on large screens (areas, plus task groups with important ones starred); a five-tab bottom bar on phones.
**Density:** low. One column about 760 px wide, tall rows, lots of white space.
**Type:** Instrument Sans throughout, 40 px page titles and 15–16 px body.
**Colour:** white with a pale green-grey side bar. One teal accent for actions; red and amber only for urgency.
