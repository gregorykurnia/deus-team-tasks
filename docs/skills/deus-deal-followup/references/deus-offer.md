# What DEUS sells

Last updated: November 2026, from the DEUS GBA deck and Greg's Mayora Indah call. Items marked **[confirm]** were unclear or inconsistent; fix them here once settled.

## Products

- **DEUS Discover / Game-Based Assessment (GBA)** - online games measuring personality, cognitive ability, behaviour under pressure and business judgment, for hiring and developing people. Most sales conversations are about this.
- **DEUS Enhance** - performance management: KPIs, appraisals and calibration together, linked to bonus, incentive and salary decisions, alongside the client's existing HRIS, ERP and payroll.
- **Potentia** - game-based cognitive assessment for children aged 7-15, for parents and schools. Developed with Universitas Gadjah Mada (UGM), based on CHC (Cattell-Horn-Carroll) theory.

More than 40 brands use DEUS (retail, telecom, education, FMCG, F&B, electronics, fashion, healthcare, manufacturing, finance). Telkom Indonesia is the flagship client.

## GBA modules

Modules can be bought one at a time; no package is required.

| Module | What it measures | Format | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Personality | Big Five based: 8 traits × 4 sub-traits (= 32) and 8 competencies × 3 sub-competencies (= 24) | RPG / visual-novel story; 32 scenarios, each with 4 options the person must force-rank (128 options). Fixed items for everyone. | 60 min |
| Cognitive | 6 domains across 7 games: working memory, attention & inhibitory control, visual reasoning, spatial reasoning, executive function & planning, plus memory/logic tasks | Separate mini-game per domain. 6 separate scores (stanine + percentile vs Indonesian norms, bands from superior to below average). No single IQ score. Fixed items, not adaptive. | 20 min |
| Neuroscience / Drive & Risk | Motivation, effort allocation, risk-taking and learning, decision making and speed, processing / psychomotor speed, attention to detail, emotion recognition (EQ) | 6 games: Balloon, Tower, Keypress, Hard or Easy Task, Lengths, Faces. Captures unconscious behaviour rather than self-report. | 30 min |
| Job Simulation | Analytical thinking, business acumen, financial ability | 2 customizable business case scenarios | 90 min |

## Platform and reports

- Browser-based, no install; managed through a CMS. HR sees who has finished.
- Anti-cheat proctoring through the camera (no stand-ins).
- Reports auto-scored, ready the next day.
- Report types: batch summary (recommended / not recommended, suitability), individual report (strengths, improvement areas, development opportunities), internal promotion report with interview question guide, profile suitability report against a role (role fit + culture fit).
- Competencies and success profiles can be fully customized to the client's framework and values.

## Credibility claims (what we can say)

- Built on established science: Big Five personality model, neuroscience behavioural paradigms, classical cognitive frameworks (Greg cites Cattell-Horn).
- 4-phase development: theory → item development → validation → norming, guided by psychometricians and I/O psychology researchers. Research partner: Ansevana Research.
- Convergent validity with traditional cognitive tests.
- Normed on an Indonesian population.
- Fixed items for everyone (chosen for fairness in a game format; not IRT/adaptive).
