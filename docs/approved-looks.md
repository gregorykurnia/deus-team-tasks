# Looks the design approves on purpose; the fresh-eyes checker is told not to report them

- Sheet action edges differ from field edges: the approved prototype uses 24px body gutters and 18px header/footer action gutters on desktop (20px body, 12px header and 14px footer on phone). Source: `redesign/design/prototype/assets/app.css`, `.sheet-head`, `.sheet-body`, `.sheet-foot`.
- A focused Close button has a 2.5px blue ring offset 2px outside the button; the ring can extend beyond the field edge while remaining inside the sheet. Source: `redesign/design/design-guide.md` §4.1.
- Sheets have a plain fixed title above an independently scrolling body, without a header divider in the approved prototype. Content can continue beyond that scroll boundary; captures must still show the labels of the controls they feature. Source: design guide §4.11 and prototype `.sheet-head`/`.sheet-body`.
- Sheets use 94% white with a 30px backdrop blur, so faint background colour and shapes may show through; that alone is not leftover form content. Source: design guide §2.1, `--surface-sheet`.
- Quick capture is a single-line input: a long example placeholder may truncate on phones while its instruction and accessible label remain complete. Source: design guide §4.8 and prototype `.capture input`; this does not permit clipping saved task text or action labels.
- Toasts stay at the viewport's bottom centre when a right-hand sheet is open; crossing its noninteractive outer border alone is not a fault. Obscured actions or text still are. Source: design guide §4.14 and prototype toast positioning.
- Task loading/failure messages can occupy the top of a full-height details sheet with empty space below; compact sheets are reserved for the specified short-sheet flows. Source: design guide §§4.11, 4.15.
